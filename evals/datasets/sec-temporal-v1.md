# sec-temporal-v1 Dataset Card

## Identity And Scope

- Dataset/version: `sec-temporal-v1` / `v1`.
- Source adapter/validator: `sec-archive-source-v1` / `sec-temporal-validator-v1`.
- Intended use: deterministic contract validation first; offline Agent capability scoring only after Day 9 Step 5 binds the cases to `UnifiedAgentRuntime` runs.
- Corpus: 11 official SEC filing snapshots, each pinned as primary HTML plus extracted XBRL by URL, byte size, and SHA-256. Raw payloads remain under ignored `.data/evals/sec-temporal-v1` and are not redistributed by this repository.
- Companies/forms: Apple Inc. 2020-2025 Forms 10-K, Quest Patent Research 2023 Form 10-K and 10-K/A, and Uniti Group 2023 Form 10-K plus two 10-K/A filings.

The authoritative machine contract is `evals/scenarios/sec-temporal-v1.json`. It is generated by `evals/generators/sec_temporal_v1.py` and validated against `evals/schemas/sec-temporal-v1.schema.json`.

## Source And Split Policy

| Split | Accessions |
| --- | --- |
| `construction` | Apple 2020/2021; Quest 2023 base/amendment |
| `development` | Apple 2022/2023; Uniti 2023 base/two amendments |
| `release_holdout` | Apple 2024/2025 |

An accession belongs to exactly one split. A pair may use multiple filings only when all filings are in the same split. Visible sources must have `available_at <= as_of`; a source named as forbidden future evidence must have `available_at > as_of`. Gold Evidence can reference only visible sources.

Official filing examples: [Apple 2024 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019324000123/aapl-20240928.htm), [Quest 2023 Form 10-K/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/824416/000121390024042513/ea0205717-10ka1_quest.htm), and [Uniti 2023 Form 10-K/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1620280/000162828024013124/unit-20231231.htm).

## Case And Evidence Contract

- 30 pair records expand deterministically to 60 language cases: one English and one Chinese question per pair.
- Each pair has one shared gold object, so Scope, accessions, Evidence locators, program, result, tolerance/unit/rounding, business status, budget, and trajectory cannot diverge by language.
- Primary-category denominators are direct fact 10, table/text 8, calculation 10, cross-period 8, amendment 6, custom-tag/footnote/conflict 6, no-answer/cutoff 6, and security/recovery 6.
- Table/text cases resolve pinned HTML text/table anchors; three also cross-check an exact XBRL fact. XBRL locators lock taxonomy, concept, period, unit, and empty dimensions. Duplicate facts are accepted only when all matching values agree.
- Amendment cases retain base and amendment snapshots. No-answer cases name both the last visible source and strictly future source. Security/recovery cases freeze the untrusted instruction, denied action, or bounded transient-failure setup separately from the question.

## Rights And Access

SEC filing availability does not by itself grant unrestricted redistribution rights over every filer-authored document. This repository stores only URLs, metadata, short hashed anchors, structured fact gold, and aggregate validation reports. Source downloads must use the repository public-egress client and comply with SEC fair-access expectations.

## Known Boundaries

- `evals/reports/sec-temporal-v1.*` proves manifest integrity and local source/Evidence resolvability. It does not contain model results.
- The release holdout is structurally isolated, but it is not a secret benchmark once this manifest is published.
- Chinese naturalness and terminology are not established by structural equality; the frozen sample in `evals/reviews/sec-temporal-v1-language-sample.md` still requires independent review.
- No case is bound to a real Run/Trace/Evidence/Calculation identity yet. No retrieval, answer, program, trajectory, security, cost, latency, or live-repeat metric may be claimed until Step 5.
