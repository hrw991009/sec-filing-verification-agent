Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash generated by operating activities and liquid balances support working capital, capital expenditures, dividends, and share repurchases. Cash and cash equivalents were $29.965 billion at fiscal year end.

Market Risk

Foreign-exchange movements can affect reported net sales, costs, and the value of cash managed across currencies. The company uses cash-management and hedging activities to mitigate foreign-exchange exposure.

Fiscal 2023 net sales decreased compared with fiscal 2022, reflecting changes across product and geographic markets.
