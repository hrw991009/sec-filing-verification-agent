## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Apple reported total net sales of USD 383,285 million for fiscal 2023 and
USD 394,328 million for fiscal 2022. The filing reports that total net sales
decreased by approximately USD 11.0 billion, or 3 percent, year over year.

Net income for fiscal 2023 was USD 96,995 million, compared with USD 99,803
million for fiscal 2022.
